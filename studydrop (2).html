    FAQ

    Q1 Do you tutor online or in person? ▼

    Both! I'm based in the Cerritos area for in-person sessions, and available online for students anywhere. We can figure out what works best for you on our free call.

    Q2 What grades do you work with? ▼

    Elementary through high school — so grades K through 12. For college advising and test prep I work with high schoolers, but for core subjects I'm happy to work with younger students too.

    Q3 How do I pay? ▼

    Venmo or Zelle — simple and fast. Payment is due before each session. For programs, we can discuss a payment plan on our free call.

    Q4 What if I need to cancel or reschedule? ▼

    No problem — life happens. Just give me 24 hours notice and we'll find a new time. Cancellations with less than 24 hours notice may be charged a partial session fee.

    Q5 How many sessions will I need? ▼

    It depends on your goals and where you're starting from. Some students need a few sessions to get unstuck — others commit to a full program for consistent improvement. We'll figure out the right plan on your free call, no pressure.

    Q6 Do you offer pro bono spots? ▼

    Yes. I reserve a few spots each month for students who need support but can't afford tutoring. If that's you, just reach out and we'll figure something out — no awkwardness, I promise.

    Q7 What makes you different from a tutoring center? ▼

    I'm a UC Berkeley junior who actually went through everything your student is going through — the SAT, AP exams, college apps — recently. You get a real person who genuinely cares, not a rotating roster of tutors following a script. Every session is tailored to your student, not a curriculum designed for the average kid.
